Good questions

Frequently asked questions

A few quick answers before you sign up.

How do I create an account?

Choose Create account and enter your company name, your name, your email address and a password of at least 6 characters.

Can I split a budget by department or project?

Yes. Allocate any budget to accounts, departments or projects and see how much of each is used.

Can I stop overspending?

Yes. Only approved budgets can be spent against, and you can set spending limits that warn or block.

Can I forecast cash?

Yes. Enter income and expense forecasts and the cash flow forecast shows your projected balance.

Is my data kept per company?

Yes. Each company has its own separate data, and everyone in that company sees the same records.
