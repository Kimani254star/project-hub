Good questions

Frequently asked questions

A few quick answers before you sign up.

How do I create an account?

Choose Create account and enter your company name, your name, your email address and a password of at least 6 characters.

Can I break a project into tasks?

Yes. Add tasks to a project, assign them, and follow them until they are done.

Can I track time and budgets?

Yes. There is time tracking for your team and a budgets area for your projects.

Is there a calendar?

Yes. The calendar shows your project dates and milestones.

Is my data kept per company?

Yes. Each company has its own separate data, and everyone in that company sees the same records.
